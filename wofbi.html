About WOFBI

Growing Through the Word

The Word of Faith Bible Institute provides an opportunity for believers to receive biblical teaching and develop a stronger foundation in God's Word.

Through focused teaching, study, fellowship, and practical Christian growth, students are encouraged to deepen their faith and become better equipped to live out God's purpose for their lives.

WOFBI is for anyone who desires to learn, grow, and become more grounded in the Word of God.

Why WOFBI?

Your Journey

What You Can Expect

WOFBI is designed to help you take intentional steps in your spiritual development.

1

Biblical Teaching

Receive focused teaching that helps you understand and apply biblical principles.

2

Personal Development

Develop habits and understanding that help you grow in your Christian walk.

4

Preparation for Service
