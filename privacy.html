Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

This page explains how Mustard Seed Gospel Church handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Mustard Seed Gospel Church — Home of Success respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the general way information provided through this website may be collected, used, and protected.

2. Information We May Collect

When you use certain features of this website, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Information included in a contact message
  • Information included in a prayer request

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries.
  • Respond to prayer requests.
  • Communicate with people who contact the church.
  • Provide information about church activities and services when appropriate.
  • Help maintain and improve the website.

4. Prayer Requests

Prayer requests submitted through this website may contain personal or sensitive information. We encourage you to share only information that you are comfortable submitting online. Prayer requests marked as confidential should be treated with appropriate care and should not be publicly displayed on the website.

5. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to publicly display personal information submitted through our contact or prayer request forms. Information may be accessed by authorized church personnel when necessary to respond to enquiries, prayer requests, or manage church activities.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

This website may use standard web technologies and services required for certain features to function properly. These technologies may help the website operate, remember necessary settings, or provide basic functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

Some website features may rely on third-party services to provide functionality such as authentication, data storage, video streaming, or other technical services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Children's Privacy

Parents or guardians should guide children when using online services and submitting personal information. We encourage children not to submit personal information through the website without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

10. Your Choices

You should only provide information that is necessary for your enquiry, prayer request, or interaction with the church. If you have questions about information you have submitted, you may contact the church through the website.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website or church services change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information submitted through this website is handled, please contact Mustard Seed Gospel Church.
