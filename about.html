Who We Are

Welcome Home

Mustard Seed Gospel Church is a Christ-centered church committed to helping people know God, grow in His Word, and live out their God-given purpose.

We believe that every person has a purpose in God and that through faith, the Word of God, prayer, and genuine fellowship, lives can be transformed.

Whether you are joining us for the first time or you have been part of our church family for years, there is a place for you here.

Our Vision

Building People. Changing Lives.

We desire to see people transformed by the Gospel and equipped to make a meaningful impact in their families, communities, workplaces, and generation.

✝

Know God

We help people develop a genuine relationship with Jesus Christ through the Gospel, prayer, worship, and the Word of God.

◎

Discover Purpose

We believe every believer has a God-given assignment. Our desire is to help people discover their gifts and walk confidently in their purpose.

★

Make an Impact

We equip people to influence their world with faith, excellence, love, integrity, and the message of Jesus Christ.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Everything we do is shaped by our love for God, our commitment to His Word, and our desire to serve people.

1

Faith

We trust God and His Word, believing that nothing is impossible with Him.

2

Love

We choose to love God and people genuinely, showing grace, compassion, and kindness.

3

Excellence

We honor God by giving our best and pursuing excellence in everything entrusted to us.

4

Service

We serve God and others with humility, commitment, and a heart that wants to make a difference.
