Administrator Security

Administrator accounts are controlled through Firebase Authentication and the Firestore administrator records. For security, new administrator accounts should not be created directly from this public website.

If another person needs administrator access, create their Firebase Authentication account through the Firebase Console and then add their administrator record securely.
